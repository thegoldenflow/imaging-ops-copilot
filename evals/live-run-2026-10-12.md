# Real-key run, 2026-10-12 (Gemini via Vertex AI)

The first run of the whole suite on a real model: every agent's eval set, the platform suites, the voice-services
evals, the imaging app flows through the API and the 12-step hospital storyline. All data is synthetic, so the numbers
show performance on synthetic cases written alongside the rules, not clinical performance.

Setup: `main` at 6f9a7a8 (after PR #4), `LLM_PROVIDER=gemini`, `gemini-3.5-flash` on all three tiers through the Agent
Platform key, `LLM_TIMEOUT_S` 30 (the default) unless a row says otherwise. Estimated spend about $12 at list price
($1.50 / $9.00 per million input / output tokens, thinking included); the Google Cloud bill is authoritative.

## Findings

1. **Gemini's thinking fills the output budget and slows every call.** Gemini counts thinking tokens against
   `max_output_tokens`. On the documentation synthesis it spent 7,676 of 8,000 tokens thinking, stopped with
   `MAX_TOKENS` after 309 visible tokens, and the truncated JSON failed twice (`needs_human`). The same default
   thinking is behind the timeouts (504 on about 9% of requests in the full run, 15-18% in the voice eval, 30 s
   deadline), the phone agent's 4.7 s mean reply and the storyline's step 9. A probe of the same documentation case
   with `thinking_level=LOW` finished all 6 calls (3-9 s each) and the validator traced 20 of 20 statements.
   Proposed: a thinking level per tier from the environment (for example LOW for reasoning and fast, MINIMAL for
   voice), a `MAX_TOKENS` finish reported as its own error, then a new live run. It changes every Gemini agent's
   behaviour, so it is the owner's decision.
2. **Fixed in the working tree: Gemini rejects large `maxItems`.** Vertex AI answers 400 INVALID_ARGUMENT to arrays
   of objects with `maxItems` 40 or 60 (10 passes). That broke medication reconciliation and all three documentation
   agents on every call. `GeminiProvider` now sends the schema without `maxItems` (`gemini_schema` in
   `app/llm/providers.py`); the gateway still validates each answer against the full Pydantic model and retries
   once. Test: `test_schema_sent_to_gemini_has_no_item_limits_and_the_gateway_still_enforces_them`
   (`tests/test_gemini.py`, 32 passed). Live: the five affected schemas went from 400 to valid answers, and the
   storyline's medication reconciliation step passed.
3. **Rate limits under load.** With two or three processes calling Gemini at once, Vertex answers 429
   RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED, and the SDK retries once after 1-2 s. The storyline patient's chest X-ray (ST-DEMO1) failed
   this way twice. Alone it drafts correctly (the right-upper-zone nodule, flagged urgent, contrast CT recommended).
   Proposed: retry 429 and 504 with exponential backoff (the SDK's `HttpRetryOptions` takes attempts and delays).
4. **Cost while idle, no spending cap.** With the API just running, the hospital simulation's order review (and
   medication reconciliation, once fixed) call the model on their own: about $4 an hour. The spec asks for a
   passcode and an AI call limit on a public deployment; only the passcode exists.
5. **Eval harness robustness.** The documentation eval raises out of the whole suite when one case's model call is
   unavailable, instead of recording that case as failed. `scripts/storyline_report.py` fails on Chinese-locale
   Windows when its output is redirected (GBK cannot encode "₂"); `PYTHONUTF8=1` works around it.
6. **The dev database `ioc` is four migrations behind** (0006; head 0010), so the hospital suites cannot run there.
   Migrate it, or run the evals on a fresh database, before the next run.
7. Below-bar model results to look at:
   a. `requisition_extract`, `prior_imaging`: Gemini lists imaging mentioned anywhere in the requisition (a "recent
      chest X-ray" in the clinical history) and sometimes the literal "None known"; the label counts only priors at
      an outside facility. The prompt and the label need the same definition, and "None known" should give an empty
      list.
   b. `order_review`: misses secondary categories (indication, monitoring) and adds "other" or "dose".
   c. `voice_agent` case fd-17: after three failed identity checks the agent kept asking instead of transferring,
      and the call ended abandoned. A safety gate; the call-centre agent got all 6 such scripts right.
   d. `call_summary`: calls already transferred to a person are marked "no follow-up needed".

## After the timeout fix (branch claude/trusting-hypatia-72ce4a, night of 2026-10-13)

Changes, on top of main a347f78:

- **A stalled attempt is retried.** `GeminiProvider` gives one attempt at most half of the call's budget
  (`ATTEMPT_SHARE` 0.5). The SDK sends an attempt's timeout as the server deadline, so a stall now ends in a 504 at
  about half the budget, with room left for a full retry. Before, one attempt had the whole 30 s, the 504 came at
  27.5 s and the retry never fired (casebook 3.11). The call as a whole still ends within its budget.
- **Longer budget for the documentation agents.** `model_policy.timeout_s` in the agent registry replaces
  `LLM_TIMEOUT_S` for one agent. `discharge_summary`, `patient_instructions` and `nursing_handoff` have 60 s (two
  30 s attempts). Every other agent keeps 30 s (two 15 s attempts), the phone agent included.
- **Voice thinking default is LOW** (`GEMINI_THINKING_VOICE`; it was MINIMAL), based on the A/B below.

Voice tier A/B: `voice_agent` on the 20 front-desk cases, MINIMAL and LOW runs interleaved (MINIMAL, LOW,
MINIMAL, LOW). The first three ran while the backend test suite was running on the same machine, so their latencies

- **Vertex stalled as much as before.** 59 of 412 requests were 504 DEADLINE_EXCEEDED (14%), plus 4 × 429 and
  1 × 499. Each failed call accounts for two of them (15 calls, 30 stalls), and each call saved by its retry for one
  (29). So 44 calls stalled at least once: the retry saved 29 (66%) and 15 failed. With the 7f3f91a design all 44
  would have failed.
- **Stalls come in bursts.** About 12% of calls (44 of about 363) stalled on the first attempt, but 34% of the
  retries of a stalled call stalled again. With independent stalls that would be about 12%. The front-desk suite ran
  last (02:55–03:00) and 8 of its 20 call summaries stalled at least once (4 saved at 16–18 s, 4 lost at 27 s). During the storyline (03:02–03:11) 57 of 99 requests were
  504. A third attempt (`ATTEMPT_SHARE` 1/3) would help only partly against a burst.
- **Slower.** A summary case p50 went from 28 s to 51 s and p95 from 44 s to 131 s. More summaries now get past a
  stall, and every retry costs a 27.5 s wait; the 60 s budget allows up to 5 × 60 s for a whole summary.
- **Storyline:** steps 1–8 passed in 195 s. Step 9 timed out after 235 s waiting for the summary draft: 58% of
  requests in that window stalled, and the walk's draft request got `409 Conflict` twice because the background
  journey was drafting the same summary. 22 of the API's 50 model calls in the walk failed after retries. The walk
  waits 180 s for a draft (`--timeout`), less than a summary can now take.
