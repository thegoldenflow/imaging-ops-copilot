## nsaids-antiplatelets
title: NSAIDs and antiplatelet drugs with an anticoagulant
keywords: warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, enoxaparin, heparin, anticoagulant, nsaid, ibuprofen, naproxen, ketorolac, celecoxib, aspirin, clopidogrel, antiplatelet, bleeding, gastrointestinal, interaction
Adding an NSAID or an antiplatelet drug to an anticoagulant raises the risk of major bleeding, most often from the gut. Prefer acetaminophen for pain in an anticoagulated patient. If an antiplatelet is truly needed (recent stent or acute coronary syndrome), the combination and its duration should be a documented decision, often with a proton pump inhibitor for gastric protection.

## warfarin-inr
title: Warfarin and the INR
keywords: warfarin, inr, monitoring, supratherapeutic, vitamin k, bleeding, amiodarone, fluconazole, metronidazole, sulfamethoxazole, ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, antibiotic
Warfarin has a narrow therapeutic range. Many antimicrobials and amiodarone raise the INR by slowing warfarin clearance; check the INR within a few days of starting or stopping such a drug. An INR above the target range with a new bleeding risk (an NSAID, a fall, surgery) calls for holding or reducing warfarin and a plan agreed with the prescriber.

## duplicate-anticoagulation
title: Two anticoagulants at once
keywords: warfarin, enoxaparin, heparin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, duplicate, bridging, anticoagulant
Two anticoagulants together are usually an error, except deliberate bridging (a heparin until the INR is in range, or around surgery). Bridging should have a stated end point, such as stopping enoxaparin once the INR is therapeutic on two days.

## doac-renal
title: Direct oral anticoagulants and kidney function
keywords: apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, renal, egfr, creatinine clearance, dose reduction, kidney
Dabigatran depends most on the kidneys and is avoided at a creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min. Rivaroxaban is reduced to 15 mg daily in atrial fibrillation at a clearance of 15-50 mL/min. Apixaban uses age 80 or more, weight 60 kg or less and creatinine 1.5 mg/dL or more: two of three mean 2.5 mg twice daily.

## lmwh-renal
title: Enoxaparin in renal impairment and at extremes of weight
keywords: enoxaparin, heparin, renal, egfr, creatinine clearance, weight, prophylaxis, treatment, vte, fracture, surgery
Enoxaparin accumulates when creatinine clearance is below 30 mL/min: prophylaxis is 30 mg once daily and treatment 1 mg/kg once daily. Treatment doses are weight based (1 mg/kg twice daily or 1.5 mg/kg once daily). After hip fracture surgery, prophylaxis usually continues for about four weeks.
