## serotonin
title: Serotonin syndrome
keywords: tramadol, sertraline, fluoxetine, citalopram, ssri, serotonin, seizure, interaction
Tramadol with an SSRI can cause serotonin syndrome (agitation, tremor, fever, clonus) and lowers the seizure threshold. Prefer another analgesic, or use the lowest dose with monitoring.

## lithium
title: Lithium interactions
keywords: lithium, nsaid, ibuprofen, naproxen, lisinopril, ace inhibitor, hydrochlorothiazide, thiazide, toxicity, levels, renal
NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors and thiazides reduce lithium excretion and can push levels into the toxic range (tremor, confusion, vomiting). Avoid the combination or check lithium levels within a week.

## methotrexate
title: Methotrexate dosing and interactions
keywords: methotrexate, weekly, daily, dosing error, sulfamethoxazole, trimethoprim, nsaid, marrow suppression, rheumatoid arthritis
Oral methotrexate for arthritis is taken once a week; daily dosing has caused deaths. Sulfamethoxazole-trimethoprim adds antifolate toxicity and should be avoided; NSAIDs reduce methotrexate clearance.

## macrolides
title: Clarithromycin interactions
keywords: clarithromycin, macrolide, simvastatin, colchicine, cyp3a4, interaction, qt, amiodarone
Clarithromycin strongly inhibits CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein: it is contraindicated with simvastatin and with colchicine in kidney or liver disease, and adds QT prolongation with other QT-prolonging drugs. Azithromycin interacts less.

## antibiotic-indication
title: Antibiotics need an infection
keywords: antibiotic, ceftriaxone, cefazolin, piperacillin, vancomycin, amoxicillin, ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, nitrofurantoin, metronidazole, infection, pneumonia, sepsis, cellulitis, urinary tract infection, indication, duration, prophylaxis
Every antibiotic order should name the infection (or the surgical prophylaxis) and a planned duration. Antibiotics continued without a documented infection, or past the planned course, are candidates for stopping.
