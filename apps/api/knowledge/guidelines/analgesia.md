## acetaminophen-limits
title: Acetaminophen dose limits
keywords: acetaminophen, paracetamol, dose, maximum, liver, weight, low weight, hepatotoxicity
The usual adult maximum is 1 g a dose and 4 g a day. Under 50 kg the limits are 15 mg/kg a dose and 75 mg/kg a day; lower limits also apply with liver disease, heavy alcohol use or malnutrition. Count acetaminophen in combination products.

## multimodal-fracture
title: Pain after a hip fracture
keywords: hip, fracture, femur, pain, acetaminophen, opioid, hydromorphone, nsaid, naproxen, nerve block, older, anticoagulant
Scheduled acetaminophen with an opioid as needed is the usual base after a hip fracture; a nerve block helps early. NSAIDs are best avoided in older patients with kidney disease or on an anticoagulant. Pain relief allows early mobilisation, which reduces complications.

## opioid-duplication
title: More than one opioid as needed
keywords: opioid, morphine, hydromorphone, tramadol, duplicate, as needed, prn, sedation, respiratory depression, constipation
Two as-needed opioids for the same indication invite double dosing. Keep one as-needed opioid with a clear interval and maximum, add a bowel regimen, and watch sedation and breathing, especially with a benzodiazepine or in kidney disease.

## ketorolac-limits
title: Ketorolac
keywords: ketorolac, nsaid, renal, older, weight, bleeding, duration, dose
Ketorolac is for short-term (up to 5 days) acute pain. Doses are capped at 15 mg (60 mg a day) from 65 years or under 50 kg; it is contraindicated in advanced renal impairment, with active bleeding risk or with an anticoagulant.
