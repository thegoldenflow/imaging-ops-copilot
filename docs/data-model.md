# Hospital data model (FHIR R4)

Reference for every hospital-platform work package (`docs/SPEC-hospital.md` 6.1). The unit of work is the **Encounter**: every clinical resource hangs off an Encounter, every Encounter off a Patient, and the MRN is the patient's primary identifier. All data is synthetic (`app/ehr/seed/`).

Storage: the default backend keeps each resource as encrypted JSON in PostgreSQL (`fhir_resources`, `app/ehr/fhirstore.py`) with plain search columns. HAPI FHIR is the alternative backend (compose profile `ehr`). Modules never touch either directly; they go through `FhirGateway` (WP2).

## Relationship rules

- Every clinical resource (Observation, Condition, MedicationRequest, MedicationStatement, AllergyIntolerance, Procedure, DiagnosticReport, ServiceRequest) carries an `encounter` reference (MedicationStatement: `context`) to an Encounter that exists. Problem-list entries, home medications and allergies point to the patient's most recent outpatient visit. A test enforces this (`tests/test_ehr_store.py`).
- Location tree has exactly three levels, Unit → Room → Bed. Ids: unit `ORTH`, room `ORTH-05`, bed `ORTH-05-A`; the unit of a bed or room is the part before the first dash. Operating rooms are `OR-01`..`OR-04` under the `OR` location (no beds).
- `Encounter.location[]` records the bed history, one entry per move; the current bed has `status: active` and no period end. An ED patient waiting for a bay is at the unit location `ED`.
- An admission from the ED: the ED Encounter (EMER) gets a bed request ServiceRequest `bedreq-<visit>`; the inpatient Encounter (IMP) has `basedOn` that request and `hospitalization.admitSource = emd`.
- Patient identifiers: MRN `system = urn:demo-hospital:mrn` (8 digits); health card `system = urn:demo-hospital:hcn` (10 digits, version code in extension `hcn-version`, extension `synthetic = true`).
- Code systems as Synthea writes them: SNOMED CT (conditions, procedures), LOINC (observations), RxNorm ingredients (medications). ICD-10-CA and CCI appear only in the 8.1 coding stub. Local concepts use `urn:demo-hospital:*` (`codes.py`): CTAS is LOINC 11283-9 with an integer value 1–5; NEWS2, bed requests, pre-op checks, task, flag and document codes are local.
- Extensions are `urn:demo-hospital:ext:<name>`: `arrival-mode`, `ed-disposition`, `expected-los-days`, `asa-class`, `surgical-urgency`, `encounter` (on Appointment), `or-adjustment` (on an OR Appointment, one per duration change: `by`, `time` in hospital time, `previous-minutes`, `minutes`), `drug-class`, `synthetic`, `hcn-version`, `name-language`, `bed-capacity`, `unit-kind`; `imaging-appointment`, `imaging-study`, `imaging-report` (exam mapping below); `age-years` (de-identified Patient only).

## Generator calibration

The generator's ground truth is in `app/ehr/reference.py` (units, ED roster, admission diagnoses with their unit and ICU share, expected lengths of stay, surgeons and OR blocks) and `app/ehr/seed/simulate.py` (arrivals, admission, waits, beds). The figures below come from detached generator runs as of 07:00 on each of the five hospital weekdays (the data depends only on the weekday), counting beds every 3 hours after a 14-day warm-up of the 60-day timeline.

- ED: about 52 arrivals a day; about 20 admissions a day (14 from the ED, 6 elective).
- Wards: 79% occupied around the clock (Medicine A and Orthopedics about 86%, Medicine B 81%, Surgery 64%); at 07:00 on a weekday the fullest ward is at 98% on average. Admitted patients board in the ED until a bed in their unit or an overflow unit is free: 3 or more boarders in about 11% of the 3-hourly counts.
- ICU (12 beds): admission shares per diagnosis in `ADMIT_DX`: myocardial infarction 0.5, sepsis 0.6, stroke 0.2, pneumonia and heart failure 0.15, COPD exacerbation 0.12; elective CABG always goes to ICU and steps down to Surgery. That is about 2.2 ICU admissions a day (11% of admissions). The ICU part of a stay is 45–65% of its length, at least 1.5 days; for an ALC stay it is taken from the expected acute stay, so the step-down comes before the ALC designation. Mean ICU stay 3.5–3.7 days. Result: ICU 64% occupied around the clock (60–69% per weekday's timeline); the weekday 07:00 census has a median of 8 of 12, is 8–10 on 46% and 7–11 on 67% of weekdays; at 07:00 on the five hospital weekdays it is 10, 9, 8, 10 and 11 (Monday to Friday), and ICU fills on its own during some planned days (late Monday morning, Friday evening).
- Why the ICU mean is not higher: a 12-bed unit with random arrivals swings by about three beds from day to day, so no setting keeps most mornings within a band of three beds, and at an 80% mean ICU would be full about a third of the time (Erlang C). The generator has no early step-down when ICU is full: ICU patients would board in the ED for hours. ICU at about two thirds full, with mornings at 8–11 of 12, keeps both the ICU exception and the scripted ICU surge meaningful.
- History: until 2026-10-09 the ICU shares were myocardial infarction 0.3, sepsis 0.4, stroke 0.15, pneumonia 0.08 (0.05 from fever), heart failure 0.05 and COPD 0.08, and an ALC stay's ICU part came from its stay including the ALC wait: ICU ran at 47% (37–58%) with a weekday 07:00 median of 5–6 of 12, while the wards ran at 85–100% (`docs/PROGRESS.md`, WP1 data change).

## The hospital clock and plan

The hospital day starts at 07:00 on the seed date (nearest weekday at a weekend). The state at that moment is in the FHIR store; the next 36 hours (ED arrivals and their steps, admissions, ALC designations, transfers, discharges, housekeeping, OR bookings, cancellations, day-surgery visits, surgery start and end, orders and results) are in the `hospital_plan` module state for the day simulator (WP3). Events at the same minute are ordered so a bed is cleaned and patients leave before others arrive (`plan_order`). The hospital clock (`hospital_clock`: `now`, `rate`, `running`, `last_wall`, `applied`, `stopped`) moves only when the simulator runs; `app/ehr/clock.py` `hospital_now()` reads it.

## Events and the day simulator (WP3)

`app/ehr/simulator.py` plays the EHR: as the clock passes a planned event it writes the change into the FHIR store and sends the HL7 v2 message an EHR would send. `app/ehr/hl7.py` (the interface engine's converter) maps each message to a domain event; `app/ehr/events.py` publishes it. Subscribers see domain events only and read content through `FhirGateway`.

| HL7 v2 | Domain event | Simulator source | Event refs and attributes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADT^A01 | `patient.admitted` | ED arrival (class EMER), admission (IMP), day-surgery check-in (AMB) | patient, encounter, location; `encounter_class` |
| ADT^A02 | `patient.transferred` | bed move (ICU step-down) | from_location, location |
| ADT^A03 | `patient.discharged` | end of an ED visit (home, lwbs, admitted), stay, day-surgery visit | from_location; `encounter_class`, `disposition` |
| ADT^A08 | `encounter.updated` | ED triage, seen by a physician, disposition decided, ALC designation | location; `change` (triaged, seen, decision, alc), `disposition` |
| ADT^A20 | `bed.status_changed` | housekeeping done (K → U) | location; `status` |
| ORM^O01 | `order.placed` | lab, imaging and consult orders | order (ServiceRequest); `category` |
| ORU^R01 | `result.available` | lab and imaging results, consult done, ward vital signs every 6 h | order, results (Observation / DiagnosticReport); `category` |
| SIU^S12 / S14 / S15 | `appointment.scheduled` / `updated` / `cancelled` | OR case booked, started or finished, cancelled | appointment, location (OR); `status` |
| (none) | `consent.revoked` / `consent.granted` | consent management (WP4, `app/ehr/consent.py`) and the scripted consent scenario | patient, consent; `category`, `previous` |
| (none) | `task.decided` | `approvals.decide` (WP4c): an approval, AI review or workflow sign-off decided | task, patient, encounter; `code`, `decision`, `role` |
| (none) | `document.signed` | the signing service (WP4c) | document, patient, encounter; `role`, `final`, `module` |
| (none) | `flag.raised` | the createFlag tool (WP4c) | flag, patient, encounter; `code` |
| (none) | `exception.opened` / `decided` / `cleared` | the Control Tower's exception stream and action drawer (WP4c) | location (and the subject's encounter or appointment); `exception_id`, `rule`, `severity`, `decision`, `decided` |
| (none) | `agent.low_confidence` | the agent runtime, a run below its registry threshold (WP4c) | patient, encounter; `agent_id`, `run_id`, `review_id`, `confidence`, `threshold` |
| (none) | `workflow.step_completed` | a durable workflow's timeline (WP4c) | encounter, patient or location; `workflow_id`, `workflow_type`, `step`, `status` |
| (none) | `followup.escalated` / `followup.handled` | the follow-up call's escalation; the nurse's call-back, closure or referral (WP8) | patient, encounter, task or communication; `priority`, `by`, `codes` (comma-joined); `decision` (callback / close / refer) |

Every event has `event_id` (stable per source message, so a resent HL7 message is the same event), `correlation_id`, `actor` (`system:demo-ehr`, or `user:<id>` when a person triggered it, e.g. a scripted scenario) and `occurred_at` (hospital time). Events carry references and codes only: no names, no MRN, no clinical text.

Storage (migration 0004, emptied by a demo reset): `domain_events` (the outbox; written in the same transaction as the FHIR changes), `event_consumers` (each subscriber's cursor), `event_deliveries` (per subscriber and `event_id`: done, failed, parked; the deduplication record).

The plan is the intent and the FHIR store is the truth: a planned bed that is taken (a scenario's patients, a bed manager's move) means another free bed in the unit or its overflow units, and if there is none the patient keeps boarding in the ED (bed request active) and the stay's events move 30 minutes later. A bed left for housekeeping without a planned cleaning gets one. The simulator writes the local FHIR store only; with `FHIR_BACKEND=hapi` the simulated day does not reach the HAPI server.

## Access: FhirGateway (WP2)

`app/ehr/gateway.py`. Modules never use the FHIR store or HTTP directly; two grep tests in `tests/test_fhir_gateway.py` enforce it. A gateway is opened for an actor (`Actor.of(user, request)`, later agents and system jobs) and a purpose module.

- Hospital-wide reads (WP5, for actors whose scope is the whole hospital: system jobs, agents on their own, hospital-wide roles): `search(type, **params)` (plain dicts, the store's search parameters), `resolve_refs(refs)` (which `Type/id` references exist), and `batch(label)`, a block whose patient-less reads are audited as one record (resource type `Bundle`, the reads listed in the reason; patient reads, refusals and writes are still audited one by one).
- Typed reads (`app/fhir/types` models): `get_patient_resources(type, patient_id, codes=, status=, since=)` (a patient's resources across encounters: allergies, the problem list, clinic labs; WP6), `get_patient(mrn)`, `search_encounters(mrn=, patient_id=, cls=, status=, unit=, active_at=, since=, until=)` (newest first), `get_active_encounter(mrn)`, `get_bed_board(unit)` (beds with status, occupant encounter and since when, plus encounters waiting at the unit without a bed), `get_orders(encounter)` (ServiceRequest), `get_medications(encounter)` (MedicationRequest), `get_home_meds(mrn)` (active MedicationStatement), `get_observations(encounter, codes, since)` (a code also matches a vital-panel component), `get_documents(encounter)`, `read(type, id)`.
- Write allow-list: Task (create, update, any status); DocumentReference (create as `docStatus=preliminary`, change only while preliminary; final only through the signing service, `sign_document`); Consent (`record_consent`, consent management only); Communication and Flag (create, update); Appointment (create as `status=proposed`, change only while proposed; booked only through `book_appointment`, by a clerk: the privileged tool bookAppointment after a recorded approval, WP4b; WP8: `book_appointment(replaces=…)` cancels the replaced appointment of the same patient in the same step, and `cancel_appointment` cancels a proposed, pending or booked one, both by a clerk, behind the privileged tools moveAppointment and cancelBookedAppointment); Observation (WP8: create and update only with category `patient-reported` and status preliminary); Provenance (`record_provenance`, the agent runtime only, WP4b); `append_encounter_location(encounter, bed)` for bed managers (`operations_manager`): closes the current location entry, appends the bed, and, as the EHR's transfer does, marks the new bed occupied and the old one for housekeeping. Everything else, including any delete, raises `FhirAccessDenied` and leaves a `denied` audit record.
- Audit: one record per call (actor and role, action and event type, resource type, resource id or the scope: patient, encounter or unit, the patient's MRN hash, the encounter, and the purpose module in `module`; see "Audit log" below).
- Access (WP4): see "Access, break-glass, consent and signing" below.
- Backends (`FHIR_BACKEND`): `local` (this PostgreSQL store, in the request's unit of work) and `hapi` (`app/ehr/hapi.py`, `FHIR_BASE_URL`, `FHIR_AUTH_MODE=none`; `smart_backend` is defined, not implemented). The HAPI backend narrows the server query only with parameters that map exactly or to a superset, then matches every result on the same index columns as the local store, so both return the same resources (checked against a running HAPI in `test_both_backends_answer_the_same`).
- The day simulator (WP3) and the generator play the EHR itself and write the store directly; the allow-list is for the AI layer.

## De-identification of resources (WP2)

`app/llm/fhir_deid.py`, `FhirDeidentifier.redact_all(resources)` before anything goes into a prompt; `restore()` re-identifies model output on the server. Field list per resource type in `STRUCTURED` and `FREE_TEXT`:

| What | Replaced by |
| --- | --- |
| Patient and contact names (every spelling, Chinese names included), staff names, reference displays naming a person | `[PERSON_n]` / `[STAFF_n]`, one token per person |
| Birth date | extension `age-years` |
| Address, phone, email | `[ADDRESS_n]`, `[PHONE_n]`, `[EMAIL_n]` |
| Health card (and its version code), other patient identifiers | `[HEALTH_CARD_n]`, `[ID_n]` |
| MRN | `[MRN_<keyed hash>]` (blind-index HMAC, so not reversible by hashing all 8-digit numbers) |
| Free text: note, conclusion, presentedForm, document content, descriptions, comments, payloads, valueString, reaction descriptions, care plan activities | every learned identifier, then the regex layer (emails, 10-digit numbers, ISO and long dates, phone numbers); text attachments decoded and re-encoded, binary attachments dropped |
| Narrative (`text`) | dropped |

Codes, ids, references and clinical times stay. Free text outside resources (notes, handoffs, transcripts) goes through `app/llm/freetext_deid.py` (WP4, below). The LLM gateway takes input de-identified either way with `structured(..., pseudonymizer=)`: it then repeats only the replacement of known identifiers (no date patterns, so a resource's clinical times stay) and re-identifies the output with the same map.

## Access, break-glass, consent and signing (WP4, spec 6.3)

Roles (`app/core/models.py` `Role`): `physician`, `nurse`, `pharmacist`, `clerk` join the imaging roles; the spec's `ops_manager` is `operations_manager`. Hospital staff are StaffUsers built from the generated Practitioner / PractitionerRole (`app/ehr/seed/platform.py`): `practitioner_id`, `unit_ids` (PractitionerRole.location), user id `U-<practitioner suffix>` (`prac-doc-09` -> `U-DOC-09`). Demo logins: `U-DOC-09` and `U-NURS-05` (Medicine A), `U-PHAR-01`, `U-CLER-01`; the imaging centre's `U-OPS` and `U-ADMIN` are the operations manager and admin.

`app/ehr/access.py` (enforced in FhirGateway; the hospital API goes through it):

| Role | Resource types | Patient scope | Views | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| physician | all | own units (or attending), encounters active or finished in the last 72 h; others need break-glass | full | Task; DocumentReference of modules it signs for; signs per the registry |
| nurse | all | same | full | Task, Flag, Communication; DocumentReference of modules it signs for |
| pharmacist | directory, Patient, Encounter, MedicationRequest / Statement, AllergyIntolerance, Condition (WP6: indications), Observation (laboratory, and body weight and height for dosing: WP6), DocumentReference (medrec, discharge med list) | hospital | full | DocumentReference of modules it signs for |
| clerk | directory, Patient, Encounter, Appointment, Schedule, Slot, Consent, Task (performer clerk) | hospital | Encounter without reasonCode / diagnosis | Appointment (proposed), Task (performer clerk), Consent |
| operations_manager | directory, Patient, Encounter, Appointment, Schedule, Slot, ServiceRequest (bed and surgery requests), Task (performer ops), Flag | hospital | Patient = MRN only; Encounter, Appointment, ServiceRequest without clinical elements | Task (performer ops), Encounter.location |
| admin | directory | none | - | none |

"Directory" = Location, Organization, Practitioner, PractitionerRole. Imaging roles have no hospital access; system actors (`Actor.system`) are trusted and audited. A patient outside a unit-scoped user's scope raises `BreakGlassRequired` (HTTP 403 with `code: break_glass_required`).

Break-glass (`app/ehr/breakglass.py`, table `break_glass_grants`; reason, MRN and review note encrypted): reason of at least 10 characters, one grant per user and patient for 4 hours (wall clock), a `break_glass` audit event, review due within 24 hours, the review (justified / not_justified with a note) as a second `break_glass` event. Reads under a grant carry `break-glass BG-...` in their audit reason.

Consent (`app/ehr/consent.py`): per category `permit` / `deny` / `missing` (the latest active Consent). Degradations: no `followup_call` -> no call, nurse Task `followup-manual`; no `ai_processing` -> template only; no `sms` -> Communication `not-done` plus a clerk Task `contact-patient`. `change_consent` writes through `FhirGateway.record_consent` (module `consent_management`) and publishes `consent.revoked` / `consent.granted`.

Signing (`app/ehr/signing.py`): a DocumentReference carries the extension `source-module` (set by the gateway on create); signing it needs that module's registry entry, a signer role in its `required_signoff_role` (every listed role with `cosign`), the patient in the signer's scope, and a person (not a system actor). Each signature is an extension `signature` (role, signer, time); the last one sets `docStatus=final` and `authenticator` (with `cosign`, the signer of the first listed role, whatever the order: the pharmacist of a medication reconciliation, spec 7.2; WP6). A medication reconciliation record (type `medrec`, or any document of the `medication_reconciliation` module) has its own flow (`signing_flow`): only WP6's workbench signs it (`flow=MEDREC_WORKBENCH`, after every discrepancy is decided); the generic path refuses it with a denied `sign` audit event, and the chart's document view has `can_sign` false and `can_sign_in_flow` true for its signers. Demo drafts: `doc-demo-discharge` and `doc-demo-handoff` (one Medicine A inpatient), `doc-demo-medrec` (another).

Safety-tier registry: WP4's `config/modules.registry.json` is now the agent registry (WP4b, below): per entry `risk_tier`, `required_signoff_role`, `cosign`, `consent_required`, and the writes derived from its tools. FhirGateway refuses writes of modules without an entry or outside those writes; reads only need a purpose module.

## Agent runtime and tool gateway (WP4b, spec 6.4)

Every AI function is a registered agent calling registered tools (`app/agents/`).

Agent registry `apps/api/config/agents/<agent_id>.yaml` (`app/agents/registry.py`; it replaced WP4's `modules.registry.json`): `agent_id`, `version` (semver), `kind`, `owner`, `purpose`, `risk_tier` (ops / documentation / clinical_ds), `allowed_tools`, `data_scope` {`resources`, `scope`: encounter / own_unit / hospital / none}, `model_policy` {`tier` (the model id comes from the environment), `max_tokens`, `temperature_max`}, `prompt_version` (the pointer to the prompt in use), `required_signoff_role`, `cosign`, `consent_required`, `eval_status` {`status`: pending / passed / failed / not_applicable, `run_id`, `note`}, `deployment_status` (dev / demo / prod_ready), `entrypoint`, `confidence_threshold`. Kinds: `runtime_agent` (hosted by the runtime; code in `entrypoint`), `embedded_agent` (the imaging systems' model calls, made by their modules through the LLM gateway), `module` (no model; registered for its EHR writes). 27 entries: 14 embedded agents (13 imaging tasks and the de-identification second pass), 8 runtime agents (the 7.1–7.4 agents with their WP4 rights, code in WP5–WP8, and the reference agent `patient_message_triage`), 5 modules (registration, consent management, bedside nursing, patient messaging, `agent_runtime` itself). Since then: the module `workflow_engine` (WP4c), the runtime agent `call_center` and the module `voice_services` (WP8): 30 entries.

Tool registry `apps/api/config/tools/<tool_id>.yaml`: `tool_id`, `description`, `side_effect_level` (read / recommend / action / privileged), `handler` (the domain binding in `app/agents/handlers.py`), `domain`, `reads` (resource types returned), `fhir_writes` (resource type -> statuses), `input_schema` / `output_schema` (JSON Schema, the subset in `app/agents/jsonschema.py`), `permission_policy` {`roles` (caller roles, `system` for background runs), `approver_roles` (privileged: a list or `signoff` = the agent's signers), `target` (the argument naming the encounter, patient, document, task, appointment, flag or unit)}, `idempotency` {`required`, `window_hours`}, `timeout_ms`, `retry` {`max_attempts`, `backoff_ms`}, `audit_level`, `internal`. 30 tools: 13 read, 2 recommend (draftDocument, submitForReview), 11 action, 4 privileged (signDocumentFinal, bookAppointment, appendEncounterLocation, sendPatientSMS). What an entry may write to the EHR is the union of its tools' `fhir_writes`; FhirGateway checks writes against it and the signing service reads `required_signoff_role` / `cosign`.

Tool Gateway (`app/agents/gateway.py`), in order: agent registered and allowed by the release gate; tool registered and on the allow-list, arguments valid against the closed input schema; caller role in `permission_policy.roles`; data scope (returned types in `data_scope.resources`; target in the run's encounter / the user's units / any patient the user may see; agents never use a user's break-glass grant); consent (every `consent_required` category `permit` for the target patient); then by level: recommend output must be a draft or proposal (DocumentReference preliminary, Task intent proposal); action and privileged calls carry the idempotency key `sha256(agent_id, encounter_id, tool_id, canonical(args))` and a repeat within 24 hours returns the first result (`tool_idempotency`, encrypted); privileged calls need an approval record (a completed `approval-request` Task for exactly these arguments, decided by an approver role, plus that person's `approve` audit event); without `approval_task_id` the call opens such a Task and returns `approval_required`. Privileged calls run on the approver's authority with 1 attempt and two audit events (`privileged_call`: approved, then the result). Handlers run with the registry's retries for transient errors; afterwards the caller's fallback, else a `needs-human` Task. Every call is a `tool_call` audit event (module = agent).

Runtime (`app/agents/runtime.py`): `runtime.start(agent_id, user=, encounter_id=)` loads a registered, deployable agent (`UnregisteredAgent`, `AgentNotDeployable`) and checks the user may act on the encounter; `run.gather(...)` runs read tools and de-identifies their resources (`fhir_deid`, learning the identifiers for `freetext_deid`); `run.model(...)` calls the LLM gateway with that token map and puts untrusted text in its own `<untrusted>` block (the prompt must carry the rule that such text is data, never instructions); `run.tool(...)` goes through the Tool Gateway; `run.finish()` closes the trace and writes the Provenance. A privileged call resumes the run after the approval (`execute_approved`). APP_MODE=prod refuses agents whose eval_status is not passed or whose deployment_status is not prod_ready; demo mode runs them and flags their output.

Trace (`agent_traces`, one row per run, references and hashes only): `run_id`, `agent_id`, `agent_version`, `kind` (run / call), `mode`, `model`, `prompt_version`, `eval_status`, `evaluated`, `actor_id`, `actor_role`, `encounter_id`, `context_id` (the phone agent's call session), `input_refs`, `llm_calls` (call id, agent, model, prompt version, status, tokens, cost, latency, `error`: the provider's message, refusal or validation error of a call that did not answer, at most 300 characters as on the LlmCall), `tool_calls` (tool, level, `args_hash`, status, reason, `result_ref`, `latency_ms`, attempts, idempotency key, approval task), `output_refs`, `confidence`, `human_action` {role, user_id, decision, at, task_id}, `outcome`, `tokens_in`, `tokens_out`, `cost_usd`, `provenance_id`, `started_at`, `finished_at`. An embedded agent's model call gets a one-call trace whose output is the LlmCall.

Provenance `prov-<run_id>` (written by the runtime through `FhirGateway.record_provenance`): `target` = the FHIR resources the run wrote, `agent` = the agent (`who` a Device reference by identifier `urn:demo-hospital:agent|<agent_id>@<version>`, `onBehalfOf` the user's Practitioner) plus every signer as `verifier`, `entity` = the input resources (`source`), extensions `prompt-version`, `model`, `run-id`. Every resource an agent writes carries the extensions `ai-run` (run id) and `ai-agent` (`agent_id@version`), so `lineage(ref)` (`GET /api/agents/lineage?ref=`) goes from any output to its trace and Provenance.

Tasks of the runtime (`urn:demo-hospital:task`): `approval-request` (input: tool, agent, run, args_hash, args, on_behalf_of; output after the decision: decision, decided_by, note), `ai-review` (intent proposal; input: recommendation, evidence), `needs-human`. Decisions: `POST /api/agents/tasks/{id}/decision` (approve / reject; an approved privileged call then runs), queue `GET /api/agents/approvals`.

## Free-text de-identification (WP4)

`app/llm/freetext_deid.py`: `FreeTextDeidentifier.learn_fhir(resources)` (or `add_person` / `add_known`) collects the record's identifiers; `detect(text)` finds the spans (dictionary and patterns), `apply` replaces them with typed tokens of the shared `Pseudonymizer`: `[PERSON_n]`, `[STAFF_n]`, `[DATE_n:D-2]` (days from the reference date), `[ADDRESS_n]`, `[PHONE_n]`, `[EMAIL_n]`, `[HEALTH_CARD_n]`, `[MRN_<keyed hash>]` for a known MRN (else `[MRN_n]`), `[ORG_n]`, `[ID_n]`; one token per person whatever the spelling; `restore()` re-identifies. A known name's parts count alone too when written capitalised: from three letters as dictionary entries, two-letter ones (Li, Wu, Xu, Ma, Hu, He) like them when they are no word ("Hu"), and otherwise ("He", "Ma", "Li", per `SHORT_WORDS`) only where they read as names: before a possessive ("Ma's son"; "He's" only before a family relation), after a title or a relation, or next to another part of the same person's name ("HE, Jun"), so "He said", "Ma'am" and "Li 0.6" stay. `deidentify(text, deid)` adds the second pass (`deid_check@1` through the LLM gateway, structured findings; hits replaced and stored encrypted in `deid_misses`). Eval: `evals/deid/` (200 notes from `scripts/gen_deid_eval.py`, scored by `scripts/deid_eval.py`).

## Audit log (WP4)

`audit_events` (append-only, hash chain) gained `event_type` (`read`, `write`, `ai_call`, `sign`, `break_glass`, `export`, `consent_change`, `simulator_event`, plus `login`; WP4b: `tool_call`, `approve`), `patient_mrn_hash` (keyed blind index of the MRN), `encounter_id`, `module`, `prompt_version` (ai_call). The spec's `timestamp`, `actor_id`, `actor_role` are the existing `ts`, `user_id`, `role`. Null new fields are left out of the digest, so events written before them verify unchanged. The LLM gateway writes an `ai_call` event per call (actor from the request, else `system`; module = the task). Admin: `GET /api/admin/audit` (filters outcome, event_type, module, mrn, user_id) and `GET /api/admin/audit/export` (CSV, itself an `export` event).

| Imaging field | FHIR |
| --- | --- |

| indication, DICOM study UID | ServiceRequest `reasonCode.text`, Encounter `identifier` (`urn:dicom:uid`) |

## Control Tower (WP5, spec 7.1)

`app/modules/control_tower/` (README there), web `apps/web/src/features/control-tower/`, API `/api/control-tower/...`.

- Boards (`snapshot.py`): ED, beds and OR read through FhirGateway as the Control Tower (agent actor `agent:control_tower`, data scope Location, Encounter, Patient, ServiceRequest, Appointment, Procedure, Observation, Task, Flag), ten searches in one audited batch; rebuilt when `store.version`, the hospital clock or the last domain event changes; the API's hospital loop refreshes after each event drain.
- Flow models (`features.py`, `flowdata.py`, `flowmodels.py`; files and report in `apps/api/models/flow/`): admission from triage, discharge within 24 h, surgical duration, ED wait; scikit-learn histogram gradient boosting; per-feature path attributions for the one-sentence explanation.
- Rules (`rules.py`): unit occupancy >= 95%, >= 3 ED boarders over 2 h, OR case predicted >= 30 min over its booking, pre-op check open < 4 h before the start; severities low / med / high; each finding carries the engine's facts, FHIR evidence references and action menu (owner roles physician, nurse, operations_manager).
- Exceptions: table `flow_exceptions` (migration 0007; the narration encrypted): `id` `EXC-NNNNN`, `key` (`unit_occupancy:MEDA`, `ed_boarding:ED`, `or_overrun:appt-…`, `preop_gap:appt-…`), rule, severity, status (open, deferred, approved, rejected, cleared), title, summary, unit, subject, facts, evidence, menu, hospital-clock times (detected, changed, cleared, remind), narration, review Task, decision.
- Narrator (`agent.py`, runtime agent `control_tower` 0.2.0, prompt `control_tower@1`): reads the unit's bed board and up to three encounters through its read tools, writes the spec's schema (plus each action's `action_id` from the menu), guarded (menu actions and owner roles, only the engine's numbers, no claim of execution, the engine's severity and evidence; one regeneration, then the engine's template); evidence resolved in FHIR before display. Tools: `explainException` (recommend; an `ai-review` Task, intent proposal, performerType ops + nurse, focus the unit's Location, input exception, severity, source, recommendation, evidence) and `createFlowTask` (action; a Task code `flow-action` for the action's owner role, focus the Location or the encounter, basedOn the review Task, input exception, action, approved_by; idempotent per exception and action).
- Decisions: approve (`approvals.decide` on the review Task = the `approve` audit event; then the agent, acting as itself, calls `createFlowTask` once per action; the run's trace records the human action), reject (reason >= 5 characters), defer (15–720 hospital minutes; a `write` audit event on `FlowException`; reopens as a reminder when the clock passes it). Deciders: the operations manager, or a nurse for an exception on one of their units. Without a deployable agent (prod mode before its eval passes) the review Task is missing, the approval is audited on the FlowException and the Tasks are written by the module.
- Patient card: read as the user (`FhirGateway(Actor.of(user))`): the operations manager gets MRN, bed, admission and expected discharge (admission + `expected-los-days`) and the list of hidden fields; nurse and physician of the unit the clinical card; outside their units 403 `break_glass_required`.
- Simulator: `POST /api/hospital/simulator/fast-forward/start` runs the fast-forward as a background job (`app/ehr/simjobs.py`, steps of 30 hospital minutes, each committed and drained); `GET /api/hospital/simulator` reports it. A demo reset holds `REWRITE_LOCK` (exclusive) and every background step takes it shared, so the reset's TRUNCATE never deadlocks with a step. A person's simulator action (advance, fast-forward, run, pause, inject) waits up to 5 s for a background tick holding the simulator lock (`simulator.LOCK_WAIT`) before answering 409; the tick itself only tries the lock.

## Durable workflows (WP4c, spec 6.5)

`app/workflows/` (README there), web `apps/web/src/features/workflows/` (routes `/workflows`, `/workflows/:id`, `/reviews`), API `/api/workflows/...`. Temporal (dev server: compose profile `workflows`, namespace `hospital-demo`, task queue `hospital-workflows`) runs the workflows; a worker (`python -m app.workflows.worker`, or inside the API with `TEMPORAL_WORKER_IN_API=1`) runs their activities against this database. Without `TEMPORAL_ADDRESS` none of it runs.

| Workflow (id = business key) | Started by | Waits for (signal `event`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| InpatientJourneyWorkflow `journey-<stay>` | `patient.admitted`, class IMP | `task.decided` / `document.signed` on what a step registered, `patient.discharged`, `flag.raised` (news2) |
| CapacityExceptionWorkflow `capacity-<EXC-…>` | `exception.opened` | `exception.decided` (approved / rejected / deferred), `exception.cleared` |
| LowConfidenceReviewWorkflow `review-<RUN-…>` | `agent.low_confidence` | `task.decided` on its review Task |

The bus's delivery transactions take the demo-reset lock shared (or wait for the next drain while a reset runs), like the API's background steps and the workflow activities. Every workflow also takes the signal `control` (retry or skip a step; operations manager or admin, audited as `workflow_retry` / `workflow_skip`). Signals carry the domain event's id and the workflow ignores a repeat.

- Bridge (`bridge.py`): a bus subscriber (consumer `workflows.bridge`) writes start and signal commands into `workflow_commands` (the outbox; id `<event id>:<kind>:<workflow id>`, insert-first, payload references only) in the drain's transaction; the API's workflow loop sends them in order (start: already running = done; a closed run with the same id may be followed by a new one, since the generator's encounter ids repeat after a demo reset; signal to no workflow: dropped). `workflow_waits` (FHIR reference → workflow, step) says which workflow waits for a Task or document.
- Timeline (`progress.py`, table `workflow_runs`, steps and notes encrypted): one row per workflow, written by the workflow at every change through the activity `workflows.recordProgress` (newer version wins) and by each activity's failed attempts (`retrying`, `failed`); step statuses pending / running / retrying / waiting / failed / done / skipped, owner, attempts, detail, references, escalation level, Tasks, times (wall clock).
- Tasks the workflows write (createWorkflowTask, registry entry `workflow_engine`, a module): `workflow-signoff` (decided in the review queue like approvals and AI reviews), `workflow-escalation` (urgent, the signer, after `signoff_timeout_s`), `workflow-notify` (stat, the operations manager, after another `escalation_timeout_s`), `workflow-failed` (a step whose retries ran out; the owner for privileged steps, otherwise the operations manager), `ai-review` (low-confidence output), `news2-response`, `followup-manual`; inputs `workflow`, `step`, `kind`.
- Journey steps of WP6 (`app/modules/med_safety/journey.py`): order review (the rules review the stay's orders; a `workflow-signoff` Task for the pharmacist from `order_review`, confirmed in the review queue or by the pharmacist's last decision on a flagged medication order), admission MedRec (the reconciliation's record `medrec-<stay>-admission`, signed by the pharmacist in the workbench and co-signed by a physician). Journey steps of WP7 (`app/modules/documentation/journey.py`): discharge summary (the `discharge_summary` agent's draft `dsum-<stay>`, signed by the physician; with key input missing, a `workflow-signoff` Task that says what is missing, and the summary signed in Documentation also completes the step), patient instructions (from the signed summary, `pinstr-<stay>`, signed by a nurse). Journey step of WP8 (`app/modules/voice_services/journey.py`, behind `workflow.patched("journey-followup-call")`): scheduleFollowupCall schedules the follow-up call (`FU-<stay>`, due discharge + 48 h) or, with a condition missing, opens a by-hand nurse Task. Journey placeholders (`app/agents/library/journey_stubs.py`): NEWS2 and coding; the follow-up call's placeholder (consent check as `followup_calls`; the call proposed for discharge + 48 h hospital time and booked after a clerk's approval, bookAppointment as `workflow_engine`) runs only when a journey started before WP8 replays. Without consent the gateway refuses the agent and a person gets the work as a Task.
- Capacity exceptions: `flow_exceptions.outcome` {decision, recorded_at, narration_source, decided_by_role, verified {occupancy_before_pct, occupancy_after_pct, condition_present, checked_at, tasks}, resolved}; while the workflow runs, an approval's actions are `queued` (`decision.executed_by = workflow`) and the workflow's execute step writes the Tasks (createFlowTask, the same arguments and idempotency key the request would use).
- Low-confidence reviews (`app/agents/reviews.py`, table `agent_reviews`; input, output and correction encrypted): run id, agent, confidence and threshold, the prompt variables and the model's output de-identified as the model saw them, the review Task, the correction (`POST /api/workflows/reviews/{id}`), the eval case id and the regression summary. Eval cases: `evals/<agent>/cases.jsonl` (`AGENT_EVALS_DIR` to move them), regression report `evals/<agent>/report.json|md` (`app/agents/evalsets.py`).

## Orders and medication safety (WP6, spec 7.2)

`app/modules/med_safety/` (README there), web `apps/web/src/features/med-safety/` (route `/med-safety`), API `/api/med-safety/...`, rule tables `apps/api/config/med_rules/`, guideline summaries `apps/api/knowledge/guidelines/`.

- Order review: every MedicationRequest (intent order) and lab or imaging ServiceRequest authored in the last 24 hours, or active in a stay admitted in the last 48 hours, is reviewed once (table `order_reviews`, key the order reference, migration 0009): the rules (allergy by ingredient or `drug-class`, blocking; interaction pairs against active orders and home medications; renal dosing with the latest eGFR below 30; dose bounds by age and weight; a test with a result in the 72 hours before) as findings with their table entry and source, then the `order_review` agent's commentary (`{concerns[{type, severity, explanation, evidence_refs[], confidence}], alternatives[], guideline_refs[]}`) as an `ai-review` Task (intent proposal, focus the order, performerType pharmacist and doctor, or doctor for tests). Decisions: accept the order or request a change (an `order-change-request` Task for the physician), reason required, an `approve` audit event; only a pharmacist clears a blocking flag. The order's own status never changes (MedicationRequest and ServiceRequest are not on the gateway's write allow-list).
- Medication reconciliation (table `medrec_sessions`, one per encounter and kind): admission (home MedicationStatement vs MedicationRequest intent order), discharge (MedicationRequest intent plan vs the home list and the inpatient orders); rows match, omission, dose_change, duplicate_therapy, new, new_without_indication (the `medication_reconciliation` agent judges the indication of new medications without a diagnosis named on the order, confidence threshold 0.7). The record: DocumentReference type `medrec`, category `urn:demo-hospital:medrec-kind` admission / discharge, id `medrec-<encounter>-<kind>` (or an existing draft adopted), content text plus the JSON attachment `medrec-data` (items with source, confidence, the pharmacist's action and reason; for a discharge the discharge medication table). The pharmacist handles every discrepancy (accept / modify / reject, reason), signs; a physician of the unit co-signs; final with the pharmacist as authenticator. Modify decisions become `order-change-request` Tasks.
- Discharge medication table (the interface for 7.3): `app.modules.med_safety.discharge.discharge_medication_table(fhir, encounter_id, final_only=True)` and `GET /api/med-safety/encounters/{id}/discharge-medications`: `{schema: "discharge-medication-table@1", encounter_id, patient_id, status (final / awaiting_cosign / in_review), document, session_id, signatures, new[], changed[], stopped[], continued[]}`, each line `{rxcui, name, category, dose_text, previous_text, change_note, reason, source_refs[]}`, relative to the home list.
- In the journey (WP4c): orderReview reviews the stay's orders written by the journey's `as_of` and waits for the pharmacist's `workflow-signoff` Task (text and priority from the rule findings); medRecAdmission reconciles against the orders written by that sign-off and waits for the record signed final. A retried step re-sends the stored reconciliation's draftMedRec call (the gateway replays it); the model is not asked twice. draftMedRec without `document_id` writes the stay's own record `medrec-<encounter>-<kind>` (create or update the draft; a signed record is refused).
- Tools (WP6): getMedicationContext, getServiceOrderContext (read), submitOrderReview, draftMedRec (recommend). Intake: the API's hospital loop sweeps new orders and stays (keyed on the store's change counter and the hospital clock); the narrator loop also runs the commentary and the reconciliations' AI step. The day simulator completes inpatient orders (intent order) at discharge and leaves discharge prescriptions active.

## Patient voice services (WP8, spec 7.4)

Module `app/modules/voice_services/` (README there). Calls are text-simulated (the browser's Web Speech turns speech into text and reads replies aloud); no audio is stored.

- Module tables (migration 0010, encrypted columns): `voice_calls` (`HospitalCall`, id `VC-NNNNN`: started / ended, language, verified patient, caller relation, identity failures, unrecognised turns, transcript, the dialog state, intents with source and confidence, actions with their refs and approval Task, outcome resolved / requested / transferred / abandoned, transfer reason, summary) and `followup_calls` (`FollowupCall`, id `FU-<encounter>`, one per stay: unit, discharge and due time, source sweep / journey, workflow, status manual / scheduled / in_progress / completed / no_answer / not_confirmed, missing conditions, language, the script, transcript, answers, rule hits, the classifier's output, escalated / emergency / escalated_by rules / model / low_confidence / model_unavailable, priority, summary, the Communication, Observations and Task written, the nurse's review status none / open / closed / referred and actions; every time on it is hospital time: discharge, due, created, started, ended, updated, the transcript's turns and the nurse's actions, and the nurse queue sorts by `updated_at` within a priority). Blob `voice_demo`: the planted cases.
- Call centre: per caller turn one run of `call_center` (runtime agent, risk tier `ops`, model tier `voice`; prompt `call_center@1`, output `{intent, confidence, faq_topic, language, distress, emergency}`) or, when the agent may not run, the module `voice_services` with keyword rules. Identity: findCaller (Patient by name + birthDate + the health card's last four digits; a scan of the encrypted Patients). Intents: reschedule (findAppointmentSlots: open times of the same service in the next two weeks; proposeAppointment, then moveAppointment, privileged: the clerk's approval books the new time and cancels the old one), cancel (cancelBookedAppointment, privileged), lookup (getPatientAppointments), FAQ (`knowledge/hospital_faq/*.md`), prep instructions (Communication `prep-instructions`), clinical (never answered: Communication `nurse-line` and Task `nurse-line`, stat for an emergency), transfer (Task `call-transfer`).
- Follow-up call: due `FOLLOWUP_CALL_DELAY_H` (48) after discharge; the sweep schedules stays discharged 48–`FOLLOWUP_CALL_WINDOW_H` (72) h ago without a journey; journeys schedule through `journey.scheduleFollowupCall`. Conditions: discharge time, `followup_call` consent, a phone number (Patient.telecom), signed final instructions (`pinstr-<encounter>`); otherwise status manual and Task `followup-manual` for the unit's nurses. Script from `patient-instructions@1` in the patient's language (`communication.language`: zh-CN → zh-Hans, zh-TW → zh-Hant): birth date, how the patient feels, each medicine and its times, side effects, the follow-ups, the patient's red flags, daily care, anything else. Red-flag rules (codes chest_pain, short_breath, heavy_bleeding, altered_consciousness: emergency; fever ≥ 38.5 °C, wound_drainage, fall, cannot_eat_drink, wants_person: urgent) before the classifier (`followup_calls`, prompt `followup_calls@1`, output `{escalate, reason, confidence, summary, symptoms[{code, quote}], quotes[]}`; < 0.7 or unavailable → a routine review Task). Escalation: Task `followup-escalation` (stat or urgent, performer nurse, the unit), event `followup.escalated`.
- Write-back: Communication (category `urn:demo-hospital:call-kind`: call-centre, prep-instructions, nurse-line, followup-call, nurse-callback, nurse-referral, nurse-close; `about` the call's Communication and Task for the nurse's actions), Observation (category `patient-reported`, status preliminary; temperature LOINC 8310-5 in °C, other findings with a `urn:demo-hospital:patient-reported` code and the patient's words as `valueString`), Task (call-transfer, nurse-line, followup-escalation, followup-manual, followup-referral for a physician). Nurse actions (callback, close, refer) write as the module `voice_services` on the nurse's authority and publish `followup.handled`.
- Demo data (`app/ehr/seed/voice.py`): follow-up stays on Medicine A discharged 48–72 h before 07:00 with signed instructions (`dsum-` / `pinstr-` of the stay, built with WP7's builders): `zh` (Simplified Chinese; a coronary stent `proc-vc-pci-<stay>` and MI `cond-vc-mi-<stay>` when the stay is not cardiac), `en`, `early` (not yet due), `no_consent`, `no_phone`, `unsigned`; call-centre callers with booked Appointments `appt-vc-NNx` (fracture, anticoagulation, heart, surgery, lung clinics) and one without. `GET /api/voice/demo`.

## Documentation (WP7, spec 7.3)

`app/modules/documentation/` (README there), web `apps/web/src/features/documentation/` (routes `/documentation`, `/documentation/print/:id`, `/documentation/portal/:id`), API `/api/documentation/...`.

- Documents: one DocumentReference per document and encounter, id `dsum-<encounter>` (discharge summary, type LOINC 18842-5, signed by a physician), `pinstr-<encounter>` (patient instructions, LOINC 69730-0, signed by a nurse), `sbar-<encounter>-<YYYYMMDD><D|N>` (handoff, `urn:demo-hospital:doc-type|sbar-handoff`, one per shift: day 07:00–19:00, night 19:00–07:00; the nurse's confirmation is the one signature). Content: `text/plain` attachments (the instructions in `en`, `zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`) and the JSON attachment `documentation-data`: `schema` (`discharge-summary@1`, `patient-instructions@1`, `sbar-handoff@1`), `mode` (ai / template), `content` (the current version), `ai_draft` (as the model wrote it), `validation` and `raw_validation` (the validator's reports), `sources` (what the draft may cite), `inputs`, `run_id`, `prompt_version`, `edits`, `edit_ratio`. `context.related` lists the sources. A draft with an untraceable item carries the extension `urn:demo-hospital:ext:sign-block` (the validator's summary): the signing service refuses it on every path (the module's review, the chart's Sign button, signDocumentFinal).
- Discharge summary content: `{reason_for_admission, hospital_course[{date, text, source_refs}], discharge_diagnoses[{name, code}], procedures[{name, code, date}], medications{new, changed, stopped, continued: [{name, rxcui, dose, note}]}, pending_results[{test}], follow_up[{code, when, department, location}], red_flags[]}`; every item `{text, source_refs[]}`. Inputs: the encounter bundle (Condition, Procedure, MedicationRequest, Observation: the admission and latest vital-sign panels and abnormal results, DiagnosticReport, open Task, CarePlan, pending ServiceRequest) and WP6's discharge medication table (final only). Without a diagnosis or the signed table: "insufficient information, missing X".
- Patient instructions content (`patient-instructions@1`): `summary` (the signed summary it was written from), `reason[{item, kind, code, name}]`, `medicines[{item, category, name, rxcui, dose, purpose, times[], cautions[]}]`, `follow_ups[{item, department, when, location}]`, `red_flags[{code, because[]}]`, `daily_care[{code, because[]}]`, `unit`; `item` is the path of the signed summary's item (`medications.changed[0]`). Codes from the menus in `phrases.py` (PURPOSES, TIMES, CAUTIONS, DEPARTMENTS, RED_FLAGS, DAILY_CARE). For WP8's follow-up call: read the signed `pinstr-<encounter>` and its `documentation-data.content` — the medicines with their times and cautions, the follow-ups with dates, the red flags (code, and emergency or call in `phrases.RED_FLAGS`), the daily care codes — and render questions in the patient's language with `render.render(content, lang)`.
- Handoff content: `{situation, background[], assessment[], recommendation[], not_recorded[]}`, every statement `{text, source_refs[]}`; `not_recorded` (from `nursing_handoff@2`; optional, so older drafts still read as `sbar-handoff@1`): the topics with nothing in the input (`intake_output`, `vital_signs`, `pain`, `laboratory_results`, `open_tasks`, `flags`), checked against the whole input; inputs the last 12 hours (observations since the cut-off, open tasks, active flags, new or stopped medication orders, the stay's diagnoses and procedures).
- Validators (`validator.py`): `TraceabilityValidator` base (sources must be inputs of an allowed type; typed names matched by code, then by name with synonyms and difflib >= 0.85; drug, condition and procedure names in free text must be in the input) and the subclasses `DischargeSummaryValidator`, `PatientInstructionsValidator` (items, names, categories, doses and dates of the signed summary; menu codes), `HandoffValidator` (numbers not in the cited sources: a warning; a `not_recorded` topic the input holds: red `recorded_not_missing`). Red issues block signing, amber ones warn.
- Agents (runtime, entrypoints in config/agents, 0.2.0, deployment demo, eval pending): `discharge_summary` (prompt `discharge_summary@1`: SEGMENT per chunk, then SYNTHESIS), `patient_instructions` (`patient_instructions@1`: codes per summary item), `nursing_handoff` (0.3.0, `nursing_handoff@2`: absences in `not_recorded`; `@1` kept as the previous version). Tools: getDischargeMedications, getHandoffContext, getSignedDischargeSummary (read), draftClinicalDocument (recommend; the module's draft, created or updated while preliminary). Dates go to the model as `[DATE_n:D+k]` (days from the admission), names as placeholders; the LLM gateway restores them. Without `ai_processing` consent the module writes the template (no model).
- Delivery (mocks, signed instructions only, by a nurse, written as the `patient_messaging` module): print (an `export` audit event), SMS (`consent.send_sms`: queued with SMS consent, otherwise not sent and a clerk Task), portal (a Communication, medium patient portal, `about` the document).
- Background: the narrator loop's `documentation intake` drafts the instructions of newly signed summaries (as the agent itself).


## AI Ops (WP10b, spec 6.6)

No new table and no migration: everything is aggregated from `agent_traces`, the `llm_calls` log, FHIR Tasks and `workflow_runs`, or read from files.

- Model registry `apps/api/config/models.yaml` (loader `app/agents/models.py`): per entry `provider` (anthropic / gemini; the mock stands in for anthropic), `tier`, `model_id_env` (the setting that holds the id: `CLAUDE_MODEL_*` / `GEMINI_MODEL_*`; the registry never names an id), `approved_tasks` (agent ids or `*`), `risk_tiers_allowed`, `max_cost_per_case` (USD), `data_handling` (`deidentified_clinical_text` / `no_clinical_text`). An agent's `model_policy.tier` resolves to the entry of the active provider and tier; optional `model_policy.model` pins an entry by name. The agent registry refuses at load a policy with no entry for a provider, or one not approved for the agent, its risk tier or the clinical text it reads; the LLM gateway resolves the model through it.
- Agent registry additions: `eval_threshold` (the release gate's bar: share of eval cases that must succeed; default 0.8), `model_policy.model`; `eval_status` gains `source` (registry / run), `task_success`, `threshold`, `mode`, `run_at`, filled by the release gate.
- Prompts: every agent prompt is `apps/api/prompts/<agent>/<n>.md` (4-line header `author`, `change_note`, `approved_by`, `eval_run`; `## system`, `## template`); the 15 embedded imaging prompts moved there from code (their modules hold `registered(agent_id)`, which follows the registry's `prompt_version` pointer at call time). `eval_run` names the run in `evals/runs/` that evaluated exactly that text.
- Eval run files `evals/runs/run-<YYYY-MM-DDTHHMMSS>.json` (`app/aiops/runs.py`; `EVAL_RUNS_DIR` moves them, and they follow `AGENT_EVALS_DIR`): `run_id`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `mode` (mock / anthropic / gemini), `baseline` (mock), `trigger` (cli / regression:<run>), `requested`, `variant` ({agent, prompt_version | model} for a comparison run, else null), `git` {commit, dirty}, `platform` (prompt injection run, workflow and simulator reports), `stalls` (Gemini runs: how the run's model calls' attempts ended, `app/llm/providers.py` AttemptStats: calls, attempts, stalled (504 or client timeout), rate_limited, errors, first_stalled, after_stall, stalled_after_stall, saved, lost, failed, first_attempt_stall_rate, stall_after_stall_rate, by_agent {agent: the same}; null for mock and Anthropic runs), and per agent: `suite`, `suite_kind` (builtin / declared / common / report), `source`, `agent_version`, `prompt_version`, `prompt_sha256` (16 hex of the system and template text), `model`, `model_key`, `mode`, `threshold`, `pipeline_eval`, `metrics` {schema_validity, grounding_rate, task_success, human_override_rate, latency_p50_ms, latency_p95_ms, cost_per_case_usd, cost_estimated, cases, cases_passed, model_calls, tokens_in, tokens_out, human_decisions}, `pipeline` (only in the entries of a suite whose harness also calls agents that are not its own, the same on each: `other_agents` {agent: {model_calls, failed_calls, cost_usd, tokens_in, tokens_out, cases}} (calls in no entry's metrics), and the whole case across every agent: `model_calls`, `cases`, `cost_per_case_usd`, `cost_estimated`, `latency_p50_ms`, `latency_p95_ms`), `extra` (the module's own metrics), `gates` {name: {value, threshold, passed, reason (why it failed when the value alone does not say; `bilingual_consistency` also carries documents and min_documents)}} (the module's gates, `cost_per_case` against the model's max, `injection_refused`), `stalls` (Gemini runs: the agent's own calls, as in the run's `by_agent`), `status` (met / below / gates_only / no_eval_set / no_cases / error), `met`, `eval_status` (what the release gate derives), `case_results` [{id, source, passed, schema_valid, grounded, latency_ms, cost_usd, pipeline_cost_usd and pipeline_latency_ms (with `pipeline`: the case across every agent), expected, got (trimmed), call_errors [{outcome, error}] (this agent's model calls of the case that did not answer), run_ids (this agent's runs of the case), related_run_ids (the other agents' runs of the same case), traces (failing cases: the trace records captured before the rollback, up to 10: the agent's failing runs first, then its other runs, and the other agents' failing runs when none of its own failed), traces_dropped (how many runs were left out)}].
- Failure detail (owner-confirmed 2026-10-13, after the live run `run-2026-10-12T225622`): the trace's `llm_calls[].error` (above, in the trace) is what the run file's failing cases capture; each case result adds `call_errors` (the agent's calls of the case that did not answer, with that error), `related_run_ids` (`run_ids` are the agent's own runs) and `traces_dropped`; a gate row may add `reason` (`bilingual_consistency`: `documents`, `min_documents`). Additive: older run files without these fields still load.
- Common case view (`app/aiops/suites.py`): `{id, agent_id, source (authored / human_review / regression), input_refs, expected}` (+ `materialized` when the case writes its own synthetic patient `Patient/ev-<case>-pat`, + `input` for prompt-replay cases). The low-confidence workflow's `human_review` cases (`evals/<agent>/cases.jsonl`) are in this format.
- Release gate (`app/aiops/release.py`): effective `eval_status` = the newest non-variant run of the agent's current prompt version: below the threshold or a gate failed → failed; met live → passed; met in mock mode → pending (pipeline evals: passed); before any run, the YAML value.
- Alert Tasks: FHIR Task, code `ai-ops-alert` (system `urn:demo-hospital:task`), status requested → completed, intent order, priority from `config/alerts.yaml`, `performerType` text admin, no patient; inputs `alert`, `scope`, `day` (one Task per alert, scope and day), then `metric`, `value`, `threshold`; written by the registry module `ai_ops` through the action tool `createAlertTask` (idempotent, traced), completed through `updateTask` with a "Resolved by" note plus the admin's `write` audit event (`resolve_alert`).
- Cost: live calls carry the gateway's `cost_usd`; mock calls (cost 0) are shown at the list-price estimate (`PRICES` × the mock's token counts) and flagged `estimated`.

## Hospital storyline (WP9, spec 8.2)

One synthetic patient through the hospital: preset `app/ehr/seed/journey.py` (the spec's `demo/seed_journey.py`), EHR script `app/ehr/storyline.py`, people's EHR actions `app/ehr/accepted.py`, module `app/modules/storyline/` (README there), web `/storyline`. The preset is applied only by the storyline's reset (`POST /api/storyline/reset`: the demo reset, then `journey.plant`), so the default demo data and every earlier test are unchanged. No new table and no migration.

- Fixed ids (`*-j0001`): Patient `pat-j0001` (MRN 40720001, 72 on the hospital day, her daughter as `contact`; the reset's language picks who she is: Margaret Ellis with `communication` en, the default, or Wang Xiuying 王秀英 with a `usual` Chinese name and `communication` zh-CN; the storyline state's `language` says which); her history `enc-j0001-clinic`, `enc-j0001-prior` (an IMP stay 210 days earlier), Conditions `cond-j0001-afib` / `-htn` / `-osteo`, MedicationStatements `medst-j0001-<drug>` (warfarin, amlodipine, metoprolol, atorvastatin), Observations `obs-j0001-*`, Consents `consent-j0001-<category>` (all permit); the ED visit `ed-j0001` (EMER) with `ctas-ed-j0001`, Flag `flag-ed-j0001-fall-risk`, ServiceRequests `ord-ed-j0001-xr-hip` / `-ct-hip` (imaging) and `-inr` / `-cbc` (laboratory; the INR result `obs-ord-ed-j0001-inr` is 3.8); the stay `stay-j0001` (IMP, Orthopedics service, a Surgery bed) with admission orders `medrx-stay-j0001-NN` (from the plan's `med_plan`: naproxen is `-03`), the OR case (request `orreq-surg-j0001`, Appointment `appt-surg-j0001-book-1`), vital signs `vit-stay-j0001-<tag>` with the NEWS2 Observation `news2-stay-j0001-<tag>` (code `NEWS2` in `urn:demo-hospital:code`, category survey), discharge prescriptions `medrx-dc-stay-j0001-NN`, the admitting physician's diagnoses of the stay: her known conditions `cond-j0001-afib-stay` and `cond-j0001-osteo-stay` (encounter diagnoses copied from the problem list, so the discharge summary may name them) and the note `cond-j0001-htn-note` (it names lisinopril, which no order has: the discharge summary's untraceable item), and the module records of earlier work packages (`medrec-stay-j0001-admission` / `-discharge`, `dsum-stay-j0001`, `pinstr-stay-j0001`, follow-up call `FU-stay-j0001`). Her imaging-centre record is `PT-J0001` (referrer `R-ED01`, requisitions `REQ-J0001` hip X-rays, `REQ-J0002` CT hip). The two records are linked by a preset MPI entry in the storyline state, because the master patient index is an 8.1 roadmap card. Capacity filler stays are `stay-j1NN`, and their pending laboratory orders are `ord-<encounter>-jhK`. Other resources the preset writes with the generator's builders are numbered `<prefix>-jpNNNN` (for example `cond-jp0001`), so they never repeat the day's ids.
- Plan (`hospital_plan`) additions:
  - Visits carry `expected` (planned, not arrived: the ambulance pre-notification), `story` and `manual_triage`.
  - Stays carry `bed_units` (the units the admission may use, in order) and `med_plan` (explicit admission orders; the random home and inpatient medicines are skipped).
  - Events carry `fixed` (a stay's deferral does not move them).
  - `bed_holds` (bed → `{stay, until}`): a bed held for a stay's admission. Until that stay is admitted or `until` has passed, the simulator gives it to no other admission or transfer. The storyline holds the bed the early discharges free for her, from its cleaning to her admission a minute later.
  - New event kinds: `vitals` (a spot panel with a NEWS2 score: Observations and ORU^R01) and `prescriptions` (discharge prescriptions, intent plan, no HL7).
  - The horizon is extended to 07:00 + 5 days 6 hours, which covers the discharge at 11:00 on day 3 and the call 48 hours later. Beyond the generated 36 hours only the storyline's events, the ward rounds and housekeeping are planned.
- The simulator's `apply_now(store, event, operator=…)` applies one event at the current time (the person's EHR action).
- Blob `storyline` (the state): `version`, `prepared_at`, `operator`, `ctx`, `patient`, `mrn`, `visit`, `stay`, `surgery`, `birth_date`, `mpi`, `capacity` (`surgery_ready`, `filled`, `warnings`), `beats` (hospital times the clock may advance to), `marks` (what the script has done: `triage_suggestion`, `course_planned`, `early_rounds`, `bed_offered`, `discharge_medrec`, …).
- Suggestion cards (CDS Hooks style):
  - The recommend tool `suggestEhrAction` writes an `ai-review` Task (intent proposal) whose input `ehr_action` is JSON: `{kind: registration | triage | imaging_order, …}`.
  - When the card is accepted, `approvals.decide` publishes `task.decided`. The consumer `ehr.accepted-suggestions` then records the action with the person as the operator (EVN-5), for the roles in `accepted.ACCEPTED_BY`: clerk for a registration, nurse for a triage, physician for imaging orders.
  - A card the EHR cannot record gets a note saying why.
  - The roadmap stubs write their cards with `submitForReview` or `createFlag`. Their kind comes from the card's agent stamp: `critical_value_routing`, `deterioration_alert` (a `news2` Flag and the nurse's `news2-response` card, decidable in the review queue) and `coding_assist`.
- Control Tower: the unit-occupancy fact `overflow_discharge_ready` and its action `overflow_discharge_rounds` (physician); the `or_overrun` rule's block-overtime facts (`booked_end`, `case_minutes_past_block`, `block_overtime_risk`, `block_threshold_minutes`; RuleConfig `or_block_overrun_minutes` 15). The gateway write `FhirGateway.adjust_or_booking(appointment_id, minutes, reason=…)`: operations manager only, a booked OR case only, 15–600 minutes; it changes `end` and `minutesDuration`, appends the extension `or-adjustment` (who, the hospital time, the old and new minutes; no free text, the reason is in the audit event) and is audited ("OR booking X -> Y min"). The storyline's step 7 takes its signer and time from that extension: the audit event's time is the wall clock. API `POST /api/control-tower/or-cases/{id}/adjust`; privileged tool `adjustOrBooking`.
- Workflow: `JourneyInput.ed_visit`. A journey started at the registration runs version 4 (`workflow.patched("journey-ed-phase")`) with the steps `imagingOrders` and `criticalValue` (roadmap) before `bedAssignment`. The bridge signals `{kind: admitted}` when the admission of a stay it waits for arrives. The journey id stays `journey-<stay>`.
- Not written: EpisodeOfCare (the ED visit and the stay are linked by the patient and the plan, as before).
